# ANCHORS

These are the certainties this person reasons from rather than about: fixed starting points that decide, before any particular situation is examined, what counts as a wrong, who is trustworthy, where the sacred is, and what they owe. Several of them collide with each other — the same person holds a settled distrust of the dominant society while pursuing standing inside it, and holds absolute filial obligation while acting against the mother's stated will. Those collisions are not errors in the record; they are load-bearing and are marked contested. Note also that the evidence rests on a single pass with no corroboration, so the singularity-derived items are candidates rather than confirmed anchors.

## A1 DOMINANT SOCIETY PRESUMED UNTRUSTWORTHY  (CONTESTED)

They begin from the settled premise that the dominant, white-controlled world and its representatives are not to be trusted. This is not a conclusion drawn case by case: it was installed early by a parent's warnings and is treated as confirmed by every later experience of exclusion, betrayal and ridicule, so new institutions and individuals from that world are read through it before any situation-specific evidence arrives. The same person nonetheless seeks and takes pride in recognition won inside those settings.
