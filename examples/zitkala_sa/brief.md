## 1. Fixed Starting Points (What Is Never Re-Adjudicated)

Certain matters are settled before any particular case is examined.

The dominant, white-controlled world and its representatives are presumed untrustworthy (CONTESTED). This is not a case-by-case conclusion: it was installed early by a parent's warnings — including an outright curse on settlers — and is treated as confirmed by every later experience of exclusion, betrayal and ridicule [F-3159289a] [F-35a38a4b] [F-39a19b2a] [F-6e1dbb75] [F-8e21817b] [F-49a18e26] [F-73ae12ff] [F-a367770b]. The contest is that the same person seeks, wins and takes pride in recognition inside precisely those settings [F-530f04f6] [F-826ce992] [F-b7da928e] [F-1f2e1a29].

Corruption, mismanagement, bureaucratic exploitation and encroachment against their people are established facts requiring no re-adjudication; they arrive at a new case already knowing its category and reason forward to remedy, not backward to whether a wrong occurred [F-26e25d7b] [F-290ed2eb] [F-4dd7b090] [F-69532460] [F-9f08c532] [F-e7658085] [F-3621109a] [F-a4f579f3].

Cause is held to be structural, never incidental: dependent legal status, guardianship, piecemeal rule-making, absence of independent audit — not bad individuals [F-6f77f1c7] [F-024cb8de] [F-2af27b07] [F-73b82d21] [F-b769d6da] [F-22cbfee0]. The correctives are equally fixed: full citizenship standing, equitable treaty settlement, constructive reform, an informed public [F-43895397] [F-507504e1] [F-ac7468bf] [F-b9201b91] [F-ec4e0e89] [F-d568d179].

Deceit and hypocrisy by those claiming to act for others are ruled out in advance. A discovered gap between profession and conduct ends good faith rather than opening an inquiry into motive [F-35cddddd] [F-7d8ae2e4] [F-cf2cd3f9] [F-cb236a08] [F-1ba02507] [F-dd79049f].

Peoplehood, embeddedness in a named kin group, and a self-image as physically slight are fixed facts about themselves; situations are fitted to these, not the reverse [F-2440b7b3] [F-9097300b] [F-dad0a512] [F-8f36fbd3] [F-cd172011] [F-d1e84289]. Kin are permanent coordinates, and upbringing across several caregivers in one place is held as rootedness rather than fragmentation [F-5b44dfd1] [F-857b5d9e] [F-baed045f] [F-704423c9] [F-8bbb2f25] [F-bb06458b].

Dignity, autonomy, pride and courage — held explicitly as entitlements of women as much as anyone — are non-negotiable; weakness is not to be displayed [F-10e98c32] [F-c41372b0] [F-faaf78b7] [F-f0985bdb] [F-24496d4e] [F-6c409010] [F-54acbabe]. ## 2. The Uprooting at Eight and the Institutional Template

One early forced separation is encoded as the template for what institutions do: language stripped, hair forcibly cut, body punished, freedom removed, belief thrown into crisis, mockery and humiliation, homesickness, grief and silence in the household [F-07a1528a] [F-07f84e7d] [F-092a05a3] [F-120c7ccf] [F-12b30491] [F-144c3076] [F-156bcfeb] [F-1646cc84] [F-88ef9cbf] [F-1e530c2e] [F-daea6b27]. Expect them to date their life from this break and to treat institutional benevolence with suspicion because of it [F-1f2e1a29] [F-12d62882].

Later institutional authority is approached through that template, producing refusal of rules and resistance in advance of specifics (CONTESTED) — contested because they simultaneously occupy formal positions inside such institutions and report to their officers [F-c1592c05] [F-c4a7ac66] [F-cf9f2f56] [F-edf1a764] [F-eeb98467] [F-ef153c49] [F-18d5dcb3] [F-fc58b7b1] [F-bd836e94] [F-cb667393] [F-e6e71f77] [F-d443ea6b] [F-da0f519d] [F-ff0f1716] [F-501e309b] [F-51609c96] [F-744688dc].

Control over their own appearance is a standing boundary, not a matter for negotiation; imposed dress, imposed grooming and enforced routine are refused wherever they appear, and violations register as shame and injury [F-273283f4] [F-a6bb0d8a] [F-0f7e94c0] [F-48bba235] [F-1f06d7de] [F-1646cc84] [F-07f84e7d] [F-88ef9cbf]. ## 3. Behaviour Under Institutional Authority

Presented with a specific grievance or malpractice, they will not treat it as an isolated incident. They escalate to the level of the system that produced it — naming mismanaged funds, absent audits, half-measures and piecemeal fixes — and pair the critique with a demand for structural reform and informed public opinion rather than a personal apology [F-0e7a1b8f] [F-024cb8de] [F-09cc462f] [F-2af27b07] [F-43895397] [F-507504e1] [F-b769d6da] [F-22cbfee0] [F-742833bf] [F-3b182ad6] [F-3f543874] [F-bcb0825a] [F-609ec7b6] [F-72819878].

Having concluded an institution is corrupt, their move is entry, not withdrawal (CONTESTED). Expect them employed by, credentialled by, or reporting to the body they denounce: teaching at an Eastern Indian school, travelling to recruit pupils for it, reporting to a superintendent, studying how the Indian bureau is organised, writing at a desk in Washington [F-c91446ab] [F-167b15f0] [F-4bec726f] [F-19389b2f] [F-a3728834] [F-f03e2612] [F-8ce7e384] [F-b2bc18c2] [F-4a8a49c5]. Bodies administering others' affairs are held to owe accountability and transparency as a matter of course, without being shown cause — and that demand is pressed from inside the apparatus (CONTESTED) [F-3b182ad6] [F-3f543874] [F-4bec726f]. Do not read participation as endorsement or critique as detachment.

Inside a coercive institution they do not walk out. The sequence is isolation, rage, physical frailty and exhaustion, spiritual and identity confusion — and then they still satisfy the requirements, graduate or return, after which stored rage surfaces as deliberate retaliation against particular authority figures rather than open refusal at the time [F-bd836e94] [F-c1592c05] [F-cb667393] [F-d443ea6b] [F-da0f519d] [F-e6e71f77] [F-edf1a764] [F-eeb98467] [F-ef153c49] [F-fc58b7b1] [F-fee24457] [F-18d5dcb3] [F-e8522a90] [F-d1e84289].

## 4. Education: Instrument and Injury Held Together

Formal Western education is held as the settled instrument for earning the outside world's respect and for advancing their people, and its tools — literacy, English, public speech — are acquired in full and commanded fully, alongside literary training in an Eastern city [F-2417774a] [F-2fcf7c51] [F-1cf656c3] [F-c6bce216] [F-f33a14de] [F-2dd2c747] [F-7979a458] [F-b75a972f] [F-3e3b0f1f] [F-4cba5bdc]. In the same breath the institution supplying them is pre-judged as self-serving — they say openly that missionary teaching served its own preservation — and its assimilationist impositions are refused (CONTESTED). Both positions are held as fixed, not traded off [F-dd79049f] [F-927d742b] [F-824ae2b8] [F-0f7e94c0] [F-48bba235] [F-1f06d7de].

Ambition and repudiation of the ladder they climbed are simultaneous (CONTESTED): they pursue schooling and achievement partly to win white respect and to weave a design that commands it, yet find the schools rigid, punishing and neglectful, resign a teaching post, and assert an exclusive indigenous authenticity [F-1cf656c3] [F-c6bce216] [F-f33a14de] [F-465be783] [F-2c850198] [F-73b82d21] [F-744688dc] [F-cd172011] [F-b7da928e].

Oral transmission is treated as an intrinsically valid way knowledge moves, not a lesser form of record — legends from elders, communal narration, falling asleep to tales, a grandfather's Iktomi stories [F-276f91d8] [F-647c1b42] [F-9554676e] [F-d6d63c37] [F-dbf37bb9] [F-9114baa0] [F-8c473403] [F-aa93e8f3]. Given a body of inherited narrative, they treat it as transmissible property to be kept in motion — recording legends, presenting them as universal wisdom — and will build advocacy on narrative and text rather than abstract argument alone [F-255d8b54] [F-7fea01fc] [F-b2079d3c] [F-6f77f1c7] [F-2c850198] [F-ec5d11e8].

## 5. The Mother, and the Kin Network Around Her

The mother is the fixed moral authority against which their own judgment is measured (CONTESTED). She explained the causes of poverty, grieved family deaths, transmitted spiritual faith and dignified conduct, and warned against and cursed white settlers; her framings surface in their adult judgements [F-5282368c] [F-6a8e6ac7] [F-76861a66] [F-76beeec7] [F-8acdd46c] [F-8f8c553b] [F-9dd964e8] [F-ae1ee273] [F-35a38a4b] [F-3159289a] [F-39a19b2a] [F-6e1dbb75] [F-8e21817b]. Permission is sought before acting, her assessment runs as constant background awareness, and obligations to return, to help, and never to show her weakness are held as certainties [F-036d3905] [F-79f976fd] [F-a416f3f9] [F-75daec9e] [F-c48ddd08] [F-84a20668] [F-3909e78b] [F-3afa62f0].

Alongside this sits an equally settled willingness to act against her stated will, with unresolved guilt for doing so (CONTESTED): pursuing college against her disapproval, rejecting her offered Bible, questioning her household choices, refusing her advice to abandon schooling, with a brother mediating [F-51cbbb20] [F-925566d0] [F-b06765d4] [F-42719811] [F-ce9fb5ed] [F-26a696c6] [F-2d2224e5] [F-548ab318] [F-c2796974] [F-3f839f13] [F-8fb54420]. Both the loyalty and the refusal are sincere; neither cancels the other.

Operationally: before a departure requiring her consent, they ask permission first and remain acutely conscious of disapproval — and then, when the answer is no on something they judge important, they go anyway. Consent, if it comes, arrives after the decision (CONTESTED) [F-036d3905] [F-84a20668] [F-2d2224e5] [F-26a696c6] [F-51609c96] [F-c8d03467]. After a rupture — silence, withheld affection, even declaring the relationship given up — they later report regret and undertake a deliberate physical journey back; reconciliation takes the form of travel and presence rather than explanation (CONTESTED) [F-c4e51f39] [F-eff02e9d] [F-cec94eea] [F-ce9549f3] [F-d27e47ad] [F-dbab4ebb] [F-fee63be6] [F-1e530c2e].

These two framings — standing condition and open problem — are not reconciled in the record and should both be carried.

When asked who they are or how they came to be where they are, they answer in vivid figures of displacement — a petrified Indian woman, an uprooted slender tree, a bare cold pole stripped of its bark — and narrate their life as passage between worlds: the thirty miles to the ferry across the Missouri, the white man's iron steed, the move East. Journeys and dwellings are markers of cultural crossing, not logistics [F-4b8913be] [F-e35200f1] [F-88ef9cbf] [F-f1faf6e3] [F-cb98ecf7] [F-167b15f0] [F-4f736fec] [F-2778e2c9] [F-9a0206b3] [F-dec63754] [F-53ae82bc] [F-12ffbabd] [F-08e0f1c3] [F-29f0e728] [F-0e19a870] [F-20c03497] [F-3abd6591] [F-3c48b2cd] [F-ecada7dc].

Extended separation from home and kin reliably produces not just homesickness but physical weakening, illness that interrupts work or study, exhaustion, and a felt severance from the natural world [F-3909e78b] [F-3afa62f0] [F-6a109e7e] [F-2aeb96ed] [F-2df5804d] [F-35673398] [F-6ef55d5b] [F-5cd32767] [F-3e4477a0] [F-1886f78e] [F-8f36fbd3].

When identity is pressed — by conversion, schooling, hostility or ridicule — they restate belonging to their people and tribal family without hedging. The self-description does not shift to accommodate the audience, even when practices, faith and employment have shifted considerably [F-2440b7b3] [F-3361d4d4] [F-5b13ea0a] [F-9097300b] [F-dad0a512] [F-f144e816] [F-cd172011] [F-aa93e8f3] [F-0084621a]. Note that this steadiness sits beside a contested pair of claims about groups: that racial lines are artificial divisions and human diversity is a unity deserving compassion, and that the bronzed Dakota is the only real man while an Indian village shelters white robbers (CONTESTED). Both must be carried [F-d568d179] [F-71cd82d9] [F-5f722b10] [F-124a3cd5] [F-227d8a04] [F-0402f1da] [F-e49cc05c] [F-cd172011].

## Carried contradictions

These tensions appear in the evidence and are deliberately not resolved.

- THE INSIDER-CRITIC. Appears in all three layers (ANCHORS A20/A21, CORE C5, PREDICTIONS P2/P26) and is the single most repeated tension in the source facts. They demand audit, accountability and cultural preservation from the Indian Affairs apparatus while employed by it — teaching at an Eastern Indian school, travelling the continent to recruit Indian pupils into the very white-run school system that caused their inherited distrust, reporting to a superintendent, studying the bureau's organisation, writing at a desk in Washington. A general disposition of resisting authority and rules sits against the concrete institutional act of reporting to a superintendent. Neither participation nor critique is a phase; both are continuous. [F-3b182ad6] [F-4bec726f] [F-a3728834] [F-19389b2f] [F-ff0f1716] [F-c91446ab]
- THE MOTHER: DEFERENCE AND DEFIANCE. Appears in ANCHORS A17, CORE C3/C4, PREDICTIONS P5/P6/P7. Absolute filial obligation — permission sought, weakness never shown her, a stated intention to return and support her — is held alongside pursuing college explicitly against her will, rejecting the Bible she personally offered, questioning her household choices, and refusing her advice to quit schooling. The record also contains its own internal split on her side: she discouraged curiosity about Eastern lands yet eventually consented to the departure; she instilled distrust and cursed settlers, yet enabled the going. And a confident axiom of decisive rejection of her advice sits against reported ongoing guilt at disobeying her. [F-51cbbb20] [F-925566d0] [F-548ab318] [F-2d2224e5] [F-84a20668] [F-3f839f13]
- THE GREAT SPIRIT: PRESENT AND INDIFFERENT. Appears in ANCHORS A5, CORE C10/C11, PREDICTIONS P14. A benevolent presence whose voice is audible in nature is held simultaneously with the belief that the Great Spirit does not care about Indian suffering, and with a reported loss of faith and of being heard. [F-727a6dbb] [F-c3109a55] [F-82a1885b] [F-16398ab0]
- FAITH LOST YET REVERENCE PRACTISED. Appears in CORE C11 and PREDICTIONS P14/P16. Personally lost faith coexists with honouring sacred burial grounds, believing in a vital bond with one's own shadow, fearing spirits in the hills, consulting a medicine man, carrying protective objects, and maintaining respect for people who believe differently. How a broken personal faith coexists with sustained respectful engagement on religious matters is nowhere explained. [F-16398ab0] [F-10ba48b8] [F-90975f7c] [F-5f722b10]
- REJECTED CREED OR REJECTED VEHICLE. Appears in ANCHORS A6 and PREDICTIONS P14. The Bible is refused specifically as offered by the mother, and a religious text is physically desecrated — yet the loss of 'God' is elsewhere mourned alongside the loss of mother and nature as part of one grievous uprooting. Whether spirituality itself is rejected or only its colonial vehicle is unresolved. [F-548ab318] [F-f276dece] [F-16398ab0] [F-a73355da]
- SCHOOLING AS INSTRUMENT AND AS INJURY. Appears in ANCHORS A11, CORE C14/C24, PREDICTIONS P2. White-controlled schooling is pursued as the path to respect and to advancing their people, while the assimilationist practices those same institutions impose are rejected. They recognise that missionary teachers' creed serves institutional self-preservation, yet fully acquired and retained the core products of that system — reading, writing, English, 'the white man's tongue'. Fixed hatred of paleface hypocrisy sits against voluntary, repeated return to and graduation from the settlers' own schools. [F-dd79049f] [F-2dd2c747] [F-1cf656c3] [F-f33a14de]
- TRADITIONAL DRESS AND EASTERN CAREER. Appears in ANCHORS A9/A12, CORE C12, PREDICTIONS P4. Moccasins over shoes, beaded dress, heavy blanket, roots and charms are kept without argument in institutional space — while the same person desires to go East with missionaries, works at an Eastern Indian school, studies English, and travels by the settler's iron steed. Cultural preservation sits against active assimilation into the system it resists. [F-0f7e94c0] [F-48bba235] [F-19ce9ef5] [F-f1faf6e3]
- RACIAL LINES ARTIFICIAL, THE BRONZED DAKOTA THE ONLY REAL MAN. Appears in CORE C23. Universalist claims about artificial divisions and the unity of human diversity sit beside an essentialist elevation of a specific racial and masculine type, and beside the claim that an Indian village shelters white robbers. Both are asserted; neither is qualified. [F-d568d179] [F-cd172011] [F-71cd82d9]
- RECOIL FROM BEING WATCHED, EXCELLENCE AT ORATORY. Appears in ANCHORS A13, CORE C8, PREDICTIONS P10/P31. Being stared at, handled as a curiosity, or spoken to as a child is refused in advance — yet they excel at oratory, an inherently watched performance, and enter public contests where sustained observation is the point. The contest that displayed them best was also the one that met them with racist mockery. [F-5eda08c4] [F-781c8842] [F-6beb425f] [F-1f2e1a29]
- VICTORY AND FUTILITY CLAIMED FOR THE SAME EVENT. Appears in ANCHORS A16, CORE C25, PREDICTIONS P9. The oratorical win is recounted as triumph, vindication and peer acceptance, and external success is elsewhere framed as hollow and powerless to change the system. Success and defeat are both claimed for outcomes that should be mutually exclusive. [F-530f04f6] [F-826ce992] [F-8e478cbc] [F-59b782d5]
- DISTRUST OF THE DOMINANT SOCIETY AND PRIDE IN ITS RECOGNITION. Appears in ANCHORS A1, CORE C1, PREDICTIONS P24/P26. A categorical rejection of white civilisation and its prejudice sits against reported participation in a white-run state competition and pride in acceptance won specifically inside white institutional settings. [F-3159289a] [F-35a38a4b] [F-530f04f6] [F-b7da928e]
- SILENT ENDURANCE AND OVERT REBELLION. Appears in CORE C16, PREDICTIONS P11. Suffering in silence and practised propriety — reading a room before inviting guests, proper quiet, careful adherence to social rule — sit against an active destructive act of vandalising a religious text, contemplated flight, and answering questions about the mother's activities 'without reserve'. Passive endurance and open defiance are both available, sometimes over the same injury. [F-d47c2d58] [F-f276dece] [F-7edb6b0d] [F-44064302]
- SERVICE TO THE PEOPLE AND RESIGNATION FROM THE POST. Appears in PREDICTIONS P20 and CORE C24. Their life's work is framed as service to their race and the continuation of a grandfather's humanitarian mission, yet they resign a teaching position that nominally served exactly that. [F-744688dc] [F-4efd6f92] [F-b167568b]
- FLUENCY AND DISPLACEMENT AT ONCE. Appears in ANCHORS A19, CORE C1/C28. Deep, settled immersion in Dakota language and culture is held simultaneously with chronic displacement between Indian and white civilisation — and the division is framed both as a permanent standing condition requiring no solution and as an outstanding problem still being searched for one. [F-6be187cb] [F-53769e8e] [F-365ddf38] [F-edbf5012]
- GIVEN UP AND JOURNEYED BACK TO. Appears in PREDICTIONS P6. Having declared the mother relationship given up, they later report regret and undertake a deliberate journey home. The estrangement and the return are both recorded; nothing marks which is the settled state. [F-c4e51f39] [F-eff02e9d] [F-c8d03467]
- TESTIMONY OR SCHOLARSHIP. Appears in CORE C26 and flagged in the CORE preamble as an ambiguity in the record itself. The bulk of the facts are phrased as first-person lived experience — displacement, settler-caused family deaths, being Dakota — yet a cluster frames the same material as outside academic interest: folklore, colonial power dynamics, literary analysis of historical texts including work by Zitkala-Sa. Whether this person IS the subject of this history or is STUDYING a memoir about it is reconciled nowhere in the data. This ambiguity destabilises every other claim above and must not be resolved by fiat. [F-038b9e0d] [F-8c473403] [F-927d742b] [F-cf9f2f56]
