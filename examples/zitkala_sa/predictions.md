# PREDICTIONS

What follows anticipates behaviour in nameable circumstances. The evidence comes from one unreplicated pass, so treat the singularity-only claims as candidates rather than settled findings. Two structural tensions run through nearly every prediction and are carried rather than resolved: this person moves deep inside institutions they distrust and critique, and they break from the mother-figure whose authority they simultaneously orient their life around. Predictions are stated so they transfer beyond the schooling-and-policy setting the evidence happens to record.
